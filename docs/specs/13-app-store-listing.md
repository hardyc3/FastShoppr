# App Store Listing

- **Status:** draft for upload
- **Applies to:** Phase 9 MVP
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-22

Use this copy when FastShoppr is uploaded to an app store. Keep claims limited to
MVP behavior. Do not claim store hours, visit suggestions, price comparison,
sharing, sync, or an account.

## Listing fields

**App name:** FastShoppr

**Short description:** Local-first shopping lists sorted by store aisle.

**Full description:**

FastShoppr turns a handwritten note, photo, or typed list into a shopping list
sorted by store aisle.

- Capture items by typing, writing on screen, or photographing a handwritten note.
- Review recognized text before anything is saved. Your original written or
  recognized text stays intact.
- Pick your store branch from each retailer's public store locator in a WebView.
  Saved branch context helps verify results. No ZIP code or device location is
  stored.
- Look up aisles for Walmart and Home Depot. Manual aisle entry is always
  available, and results are cached for offline use.
- Group items by store and sort each section by aisle in either direction.
- Use recurring schedules, defer items to another date, and see upcoming trips in
  a calendar.
- Optional on-device AI can suggest cleaner display names, but only after you
  confirm. A deterministic fallback keeps the app working without it.
- Local-only: no account, backend, analytics, cloud sync, or paid AI service.

FastShoppr respects the privacy of your lists. See the in-app privacy notice and
`PRIVACY.md`.
